❓ Quick Help

Common Questions

On the Login screen, tap "Forgot Password?". Enter your registered email address and tap "Send Reset Link". You will receive a password reset email from Firebase Authentication. Follow the link to set a new password.
If you signed in with the same account, your notes, bookmarks, and highlights should automatically restore from the cloud (Firebase Firestore) after login. Ensure you are signed in with the same email or Google account you used previously. If data is still missing, contact us.
Ensure that notification permissions are granted for Tamil Bible App in your Android system settings. Check Settings → Apps → Tamil Bible App → Notifications. Also verify that Do Not Disturb mode is not blocking notifications. If the issue persists, log out and back in to refresh your notification token.
In the Prayer Requests screen, when creating a new request, enable the "Anonymous" toggle before submitting. Your name and profile photo will be hidden from other users. Your identity is still linked to the request internally for moderation purposes.
Use the in-app Feedback feature (accessible from the Home screen or side menu). Select "Bug Report" or "Correction" as the feedback type. Alternatively, use the Contact form on this page or email us directly at rprpradeesh@gmail.com. Please include the specific book, chapter, and verse when reporting Bible content errors.
