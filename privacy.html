This Privacy Policy describes how Tamil Bible App ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by RPR Development (package name: com.rpr.tamil_bible_app), collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android application and this website.

By downloading or using Tamil Bible App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Registration: When you register using Email and Password, we collect your display name, email address, and a hashed password managed by Firebase Authentication.
  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google display name, email address, and Google profile photo URL from Google's OAuth service.
  • Profile Information: You may optionally provide your profession and date of birth in your profile settings. You may upload a profile photo, which is stored on Cloudinary.
  • Notes: Text notes and audio recordings you create are stored in Firebase Firestore and associated with your account.
  • Prayer Requests: Prayer requests you submit (including whether they are submitted anonymously) are stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Feedback: Bug reports, suggestions, corrections, and feature requests you submit are stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Bookmarks & Highlights: Bible verses you bookmark or highlight are stored in Firebase Firestore, linked to your account.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Firebase Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as screen views, session duration, and feature usage to improve the app experience.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic data to identify and fix technical issues.
  • Device Information: Platform (Android), device model, and app version information may be collected to provide technical support.
  • Reading History: Your Bible reading progress (book, chapter, verse) is stored locally and synced to Firebase Firestore to restore your reading position.
  • Push Notification Token: Your device push token (managed by OneSignal) is used to send you notifications you have opted into.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your user account
  • To sync your notes, bookmarks, highlights, and reading progress across sessions
  • To display your profile information (name, photo, role) within the app
  • To deliver daily verses, ministry announcements, and updates published by the admin team
  • To send you push notifications for prayer requests, announcements, and reminders (only with your permission)
  • To process and respond to your feedback and support requests
  • To display prayer requests to the community (public requests only; anonymous requests hide your identity)
  • To analyse app usage patterns to improve features and fix bugs (via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics)
  • To assign and enforce user roles (User, Admin, Super Admin) for access control

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All personal data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, protected by role-based Firestore Security Rules.
  • Users can only read and write their own data. Admins have elevated read access for moderation purposes only.
  • User role fields are write-protected — users cannot modify their own role to escalate privileges.
  • Profile images are stored on Cloudinary over HTTPS with secure access URLs.
  • Cached data (profile image, name, role) may be stored locally using SharedPreferences for performance.
  • Audio notes are stored securely in Firebase Firestore with Cloudinary URLs.
  • We do not store passwords in plain text. Firebase Authentication handles all credential management.
  • While we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update your display name, profile photo, profession, and date of birth directly within the app Profile screen.
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data. See our Account Deletion Policy for details.
  • Notification Opt-Out: You can disable push notifications at any time from the app's Notification Settings or your device's system settings.
  • Anonymous Prayers: You may submit prayer requests anonymously without revealing your identity to other users.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at rprpradeesh@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Tamil Bible App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at rprpradeesh@gmail.com and we will delete the information promptly.

7. Data Retention

  • Your account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Notes, bookmarks, highlights, reading history, reminders, and prayer requests are retained until you delete them or request account deletion.
  • Upon account deletion, personal data is permanently removed from Firebase Authentication and Firestore within 30 days.
  • Cloudinary profile images are deleted upon account deletion request.
  • Anonymised analytics data collected by Firebase Analytics may be retained longer as it cannot be linked to an individual.
  • Crash reports collected by Firebase Crashlytics are retained for diagnostic purposes for up to 90 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify users through a push notification or an in-app announcement. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
