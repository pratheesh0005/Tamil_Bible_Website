About Tamil Bible App

A modern, fast, and feature-rich digital Bible ecosystem built for Tamil-speaking Christians around the globe.

✦ Our Mission

Empowering Believers Through Technology

The Tamil Bible App was created with a single vision: to provide a beautifully crafted, fast, and reliable digital Bible application tailored specifically for the Tamil Christian community. Whether you are reading daily scriptures, studying with parallel translations, tracking reading plans, listening to Audio Bible, or finding hymn lyrics during church service — our app is built to serve your daily devotional needs.

The ecosystem comprises both the Tamil Bible Mobile App for believers and the Tamil Bible Admin Dashboard for real-time community management and announcement publishing.

Key App Capabilities

📖

66 Books Offline

Full Old and New Testaments in Tamil and English with instant book/chapter/verse switching and zero internet dependency.

🔍

Smart Tamil Search

Search by verse text, keywords, song numbers, or filter songs by initial Tamil letter prefixes (e.g. அ, ஆ, இ).

🏆

Completion Certificates

Track actual reading progress, edit display reading times, and generate printable high-res 3000px+ PNG certificates.

🎵

Christian Hymns Library

Extensive collection of Tamil Christian song lyrics with category filtering, numeric lookup, and custom song sheets.

🙏

Prayer Requests & Alarms

Share prayer requests with the community, set daily prayer reminders, and receive push notifications.

📝

Cloud Notes & Highlights

Write rich notes with custom backgrounds, highlight verses in vibrant colors, and sync across all your devices.
