2. Use of the App

Tamil Bible App is provided free of charge as a ministry resource for the Tamil Christian community. The app allows you to:

  • Read the complete Bible in Tamil (all 66 books) and navigate by book, chapter, and verse
  • Listen to the Audio Bible with playback controls
  • Search the Bible using text or voice search
  • Create, edit, and export text notes and audio recordings
  • Bookmark and highlight Bible verses
  • Submit and view prayer requests in the community
  • Set prayer and Bible reading reminders
  • Browse Tamil Christian songs and convention songs
  • Explore the Bible Dictionary for word meanings
  • Create Bible quote images and share them
  • View daily verses and ministry updates published by the admin team

  • Manage your user profile including photo, name, profession, and date of birth

The app is available on Android only (minimum SDK as specified in the Android manifest).
