📖 Bible Reading & Smart Search

Yes! All 66 books of the Bible (Old Testament and New Testament) in Tamil and English are stored locally on your device. You can read, search, bookmark, and highlight verses without an internet connection. Online connection is only required for syncing notes across devices or receiving live announcements.
When searching for songs or Bible topics starting with Tamil characters (e.g. அ, ஆ, இ), our search engine specifically filters items whose titles begin with that exact letter prefix. This avoids unwanted matches from lyrics or stanzas.
In the Christian & Convention Songs section, type any song number (e.g. 15, 100). The app will instantly display ONLY Song #15, skipping lyric numbers or stanzas.

🏆 Reading Plans & Completion Certificates

Plan completion depends strictly on actual finished chapters. Completion percentage is calculated from your verified finished days divided by the total plan duration. You can also customize the reading time displayed on your certificate.
Yes! An "Edit Reading Time" button is available on the certificate screen outside the export boundary. You can enter a custom reading time (e.g., "45 Minutes", "1 Hour 20 Minutes") specifically for display on your certificate. Editing this value does NOT alter your actual Firestore reading stats or daily history.
Once you finish a reading plan, tap "Download PNG" or "Share Certificate". The app captures a high-resolution 3000px+ PNG image complete with your profile photo, gold seal, certificate ID, and official brand logo.

👤 Account, Notes & Customization
