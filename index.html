✦ Everything You Need

Packed with Powerful Features

Every feature is built specifically for Tamil Christians to read, grow, and connect in their faith journey.

📖

Full Tamil Bible

Complete Old and New Testament — all 66 books in Tamil with verse-by-verse reading, chapter navigation, and book picker.

🎧

Audio Bible

Listen to the Bible being read aloud. Control playback speed, enable auto-scroll, and follow along with the text simultaneously.

🎙️

Voice Search

Search the entire Bible hands-free using voice input. Find verses by speaking keywords or phrases in Tamil or English.

📝

Text & Audio Notes

Write rich text notes with the built-in editor, or record voice memos as audio notes. Export to PDF or DOCX. Synced securely to the cloud.

🙏

Prayer Requests

Share prayer requests with fellow believers and intercede for others. Submit requests, pray for others, like, and respond in a spirit of love and fellowship.

⏰

Prayer Reminders

Set daily prayer reminders for prayer time, Bible reading, church attendance, and custom schedules with alarm sounds.

🎵

Christian Songs

Browse a curated library of Tamil Christian songs and convention songs. Copy lyrics, share, and export. Organized by category.

📚

Bible Dictionary

Deep-dive into biblical words with Tamil explanations, English verse cross-references, Old and New Testament occurrences, and related words.

🔖

Highlights & Bookmarks

Highlight favourite verses in colour and bookmark chapters for quick access. All saved securely in your personal cloud library.

🖼️

Bible Quote Cards

Create stunning Bible verse quote images with custom fonts, backgrounds, and themes. Share directly to WhatsApp, Telegram, or social media.

🌅

Daily Verse

Start every day with an admin-curated daily Bible verse displayed beautifully on your home screen. Refreshed daily by the ministry team.

🌐

Tamil & English

Full Tamil language support with an English language option. Switch between languages instantly from the profile settings.

📢

Updates & Announcements

Receive ministry announcements, pinned updates, video and image content from your church or ministry admin directly in the app.

🔔

Push Notifications

Stay connected with real-time push notifications via OneSignal for prayer requests, announcements, and ministry updates.

💬

Feedback & Support

Submit bug reports, suggestions, corrections, and feature requests directly within the app. Our team reviews every submission.

🔐

Secure Cloud Account

Sign in with Email or Google. All your notes, bookmarks, highlights, and preferences sync across devices securely via Firebase.

Frequently Asked Questions

Absolutely. Your personal data (notes, bookmarks, highlights, prayer requests) is stored securely in Firebase Firestore with role-based security rules. We never sell your data to third parties. Profile images are hosted on Cloudinary with secure URLs. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
