Account Deletion Policy

Understand how to delete your Tamil Bible App account and what happens to your data.

🟢 Last updated: July 2026

At Tamil Bible App, we respect your right to control your personal data. This page explains how to request the deletion of your account and all associated data, what data is deleted, what may be retained, and the processing timeline.

⚠️
This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Once your account deletion request is confirmed and processed, all your personal data will be permanently erased. Your notes, audio recordings, bookmarks, highlights, prayer requests, and profile information cannot be recovered.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion through any of the following methods:

1

Submit a Request (Recommended)

Fill out the Account Deletion Request form below on this page. Provide your registered email address and a brief reason. Our team will verify your identity and process the deletion.

2

Email Us Directly

Send an email to rprpradeesh@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Include your full name and registered email.

3

Verification

We will verify your identity by sending a confirmation email to your registered address. You must confirm the deletion request from that email before processing begins.

4

Processing

Upon confirmation, your account deletion will be processed within 30 days. You will receive a final confirmation email when the deletion is complete.

What Data Gets Deleted

Upon successful account deletion, the following data is permanently removed:

Firebase Authentication

  • Your email address and hashed password (Email/Password accounts)
  • Your Google OAuth identity link (Google Sign-In accounts)
  • Your unique Firebase UID
  • All authentication session tokens

Firestore Database (Cloud)

  • Your complete user profile document (display name, email, profession, date of birth, role, profile photo URL, created time, updated time)
  • All personal text notes created in the app
  • All audio note records and associated Cloudinary URLs
  • All Bible verse bookmarks
  • All Bible verse highlights and highlight colours
  • All prayer requests submitted by you (including anonymous submissions linked to your UID)
  • All feedback submissions (bug reports, suggestions, corrections, feature requests)
  • Your complete reading history (book, chapter, verse progress)
  • All prayer reminders you configured
  • All personal app settings and preferences
  • Any likes or interactions on community content

Cloudinary (Profile Images)

  • Your profile photo uploaded to Cloudinary is deleted from cloud storage
  • The Cloudinary image URL stored in Firestore is also removed

OneSignal (Push Notifications)

  • Your device push notification token is removed from OneSignal
  • You will no longer receive push notifications after deletion

Local Device

  • Cached profile data stored in SharedPreferences on the device (name, role, profile image) is cleared automatically upon logout following account deletion
  • Audio recordings saved locally on the device will need to be manually deleted from your device storage

What Data May Be Retained

The following data may be retained after account deletion for the reasons specified:

  • Firebase Analytics data: Anonymised, aggregate usage statistics that cannot be linked to your individual account may be retained for analytical purposes.
  • Firebase Crashlytics reports: Crash reports collected before your deletion request may be retained for up to 90 days for technical stability analysis. These reports do not contain personally identifiable information.
  • Public community content: If you submitted prayer requests that were set to "public" (non-anonymous), those entries will be deleted. Anonymous prayer request data that does not contain identifying information may be retained in aggregated form.
  • Legal compliance: We may retain minimal data if required by applicable law, legal proceedings, or to resolve disputes.

Processing Timeline

✓

Day 1 — Request Received

We receive your deletion request and send an identity verification email to your registered address.

✉

Within 48 Hours — Confirmation Required

You must confirm the deletion by clicking the link in the verification email we send. Requests not confirmed within 7 days will expire.

⚙

Within 30 Days — Deletion Processed

After confirmation, all associated data is permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloudinary, and OneSignal within 30 days.

🗑

Completion — Final Confirmation Sent

You receive a final confirmation email once your account and all data have been permanently deleted.
